Delete your cue account

In the app

  1. Open cue and tap the settings button (the gear, top right).
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account, then Delete account again to confirm.
  3. Choose your Google account when asked. This confirms it's you.

Your account and its cloud data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have cue installed, email cueapp.hello@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you used with cue, with the subject "Delete my cue account". Your account and its data will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a confirmation by email.

What is deleted

  • Your cue account (the sign-in linked to your Google account).
  • Every event saved in the cloud, with its posters and ticket files.

Nothing is kept on cue's side after deletion. Events saved on your phone are not affected; they are removed when you uninstall the app. Your Google account itself is not deleted.
